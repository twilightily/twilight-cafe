# Hi! I'm Ananya Abhilash

<p>
            Also known as twilight! I see tech as a form of art<br>
            and well anyone that knows me, knows that I LOVE art (checkout the art tab for more)<br>
            I'm passionate about cool things. I'm on a mission to change the way AI is used<br>
            all over the world cause it definitely isn't ideal right now :D
        </p>

I'm currently a second year CSE student at PES University. I'm part of two awesome tech clubs at uni i.e. ACM PESU ECC Branch and HSP. I'm infact the Design Lead in HSP'27 core! 
HSP which was once just a college club has since then grown into Homebrew, a FOSS community open to everyone! My core team and I have thus far put a lot of effort into getting this community up and I must say it is incredibly fun watching it grow.

So far most of my work has been a really nice blend of both design and tech. For example, I built and deployed my first web app on Streamlit called Wordle Unhinged, a fun twist to the classic Wordle. I also was a mentee at Tilde 5.0 (a summer mentorship program conducted by Homebrew) where I built MuSSHroom, an SSH chat server written in Go with a VERY pretty TUI created using BubbleTea framework 

Art is something that has been with me since the beginning of time. Ofcourse I was a traditional artist at first workign with poster paints, colour pencils and JUST pencils xD however around lockdown time, I dabbled into digital art! I started out on IbisPaintX on literally my phone and my finger (no stylus). Overtime I built my expertise in drawing onscreen and it has been quite the journey
Take a look at all my art and design work in the Art&Design tab :D

I've also done quite a lot of design work for HSP and ACM, mainly event posters spawned on canva/figma with backgrounds created from scratch by me on IbisPaintX

One thing that's for sure though, I'm very much against using AI in any of the work I do (ESPECIALLY creative work). It is indeed a helpful tool, however I feel like it takes the joy of learning something new out of all our daily lives. I truly believe in using it as a tool to learn rather than a tool to do everything for you
If you keep escaping things what are you escaping for?

(might I add, the posters designed by AI make me nauseous because :p)

This site came about me wanting a place to show all my work, which spans across a multitude of things ranging from design, tech, art, photos (maybe I'll add a poems section idk this site is a work in progress that keeps building itself :D )
